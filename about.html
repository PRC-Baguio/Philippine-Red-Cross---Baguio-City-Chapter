Our foundation

Seven Fundamental Principles

Humanity

Prevent and alleviate human suffering wherever it may be found.

Impartiality

Provide assistance without discrimination, guided by need.

Neutrality

Do not take sides in hostilities or controversies.

Independence

Maintain autonomy while acting in accordance with the law.

Voluntary Service

Serve voluntarily, without the desire for gain.

Unity

Maintain one Red Cross or Red Crescent society in each country, open to all.

Universality

Share equal status and responsibilities across the international movement.
