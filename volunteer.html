Volunteer with us

Put care into action.

Volunteers are part of the humanitarian work of the Philippine Red Cross. Contact the Baguio City Chapter to learn about current local opportunities.

Ways to take part

Volunteer service areas

  • Disaster preparedness and response
  • Health and welfare activities
  • Blood donor recruitment and activities
  • Safety services and community education
  • Red Cross Youth and school-based engagement

These are general service areas. Current Chapter opportunities and training prerequisites must be confirmed.

Why volunteer?

Contribute time and skills to humanitarian service, learn alongside others, and support communities facing difficulty.

Before applying

Requirements and qualifications

Age, skills, time commitment, screening, and training requirements depend on the assignment. The Chapter will provide current requirements before registration.

Application

Submit an application request.

The Chapter will review your information and contact you about the next steps. Submitting the form below does not confirm acceptance or assignment.
