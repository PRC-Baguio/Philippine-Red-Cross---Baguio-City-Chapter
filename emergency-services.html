Emergency and medical services

Service information, confirmed directly.

Contact the Baguio City Chapter to ask about ambulance and medical services. Availability, rates, and response arrangements are not published until officially confirmed.

Important: No Chapter emergency hotline or service availability has been supplied for publication. Do not rely on this page as an emergency dispatch channel.

Ambulance services

Ambulance patient transport and conduction may be part of Red Cross services. Whether a vehicle or crew is available for a particular request must be confirmed by the Chapter.

Rates, service area, and scheduling: to be confirmed.

Medical standby and event coverage

Medical standby or event medical coverage requests require advance coordination and confirmation.

Staffing, equipment, lead time, and rates: to be confirmed by the Chapter.

Request information

How to inquire

  1. Email the Chapter to ask whether the requested service is available.
  2. Include the requested date, general pickup and destination details, and a way for staff to reply.
  3. Wait for written confirmation of availability, instructions, and any applicable charges.

Do not send sensitive medical or identity information through an unverified email channel.

Frequently asked questions

What to know

Is an ambulance available now?

Availability is not confirmed on this page. Contact the Chapter through its verified official channels for service inquiries.

What are the rates?

Rates have not been supplied for publication. Ask the Chapter for current written rates before making arrangements.

Can I request event medical coverage?

Contact the Chapter to ask about service scope, availability, requirements, and lead time. No booking is confirmed until the Chapter confirms it.
